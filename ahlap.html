About this self-assessment

The Allied Health Leadership and Advancing Practice Framework helps you reflect on the clinical and non-clinical capabilities you need to advance your practice and your leadership. It describes how allied health professionals progressively expand their sphere of influence — from leading themselves, to leading others, to leading innovations, to leading systems.

This tool walks you through the framework's assessment process:

  1. Framework Assessment Tree — a few short questions to identify which stage you are working within.
  2. Self-reflection — rate how strongly you agree with each statement for that stage.
  3. Capability Compass — see your average score in each of the six domains plotted visually.
  4. Supervisor review — discuss your results, strengths and development areas with your supervisor.
  5. Work-Integrated Learning Plan — record the development activities you will pursue.

Scores are not performance indicators. This is a reflective tool to help you and your supervisor identify strengths and target areas for development. Your answers stay in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
